# Criteria: technology for older adults

(Distilled from real phone research for users aged 75+, 2026.)

## Software outweighs hardware
- **Samsung** wins on native Easy Mode (giant icons/text, accidental-touch delay) + 6 years of updates even on lower mid-range (A series 2025+).
- **Xiaomi/HyperOS ruled out**: ads inside system apps → accidental taps and subscriptions.
- **Motorola** (pure Android) is plan B.

## Smoothness floor (2026)
- Acceptable: Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 / Dimensity 7300+ / Helio G99, always with 8 GB RAM.
- Blacklist: SD 685/680, Dimensity 7025/7060, Unisoc, Helio G85-G88, and any 4 GB variant.

## Details that matter more than for other profiles
- Speaker: mono vs stereo matters (call volume and clarity).
- Display: high brightness and a usable maximum font size.
- In-person warranty in the user's city when possible.

## Delivery
Hand it over configured: Easy Mode on, 3-button navigation, Play Store PIN, and TeamViewer QuickSupport installed for family remote support.
