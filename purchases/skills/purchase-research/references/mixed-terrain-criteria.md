# Criteria: electric mobility for mixed terrain (city + unpaved)

(Distilled from real e-scooter research in Andean climate, 2026.)

## What to weigh
- Suspension (indispensable on unpaved roads).
- Tires: size and type — knobby for trails.
- Motor power and **dual motor** (decisive on unpaved climbs with mud).
- IP rating: rainy Andean climate demands IPX5+.
- **Real** range, not brochure range.

## Real range
- ≈ 50-60% of the official figure (brochures measure at a constant 15 km/h on a bench).
- On unpaved climbs, discount an additional 40-50%.

## Regulation (Colombia)
- >25 km/h exceeds the micromobility limit on public roads (VELMPU law) — note it in the report when applicable.

## After-sales
- Verify a living local distributor (the brand's official domain active in the country) before recommending — no distributor means no spare parts, and for niche brands that kills the product.
